# M04 Personal video (travel, lifestyle, portraits, family, personal cinematic)

Auto-detect: "travel", "vlog", "my trip", "family", "wedding", "portrait", "day in my life".
Workflow: `workflows/personal_video_workflow.md`. Adapter: `adapters/physical_camera.md` (phone or camera) and `adapters/edit_resolve.md`.

## Priorities
1. Emotional authenticity: real moments over staged ones. Do not make it look staged unless the user asks.
2. Natural movement: handheld or gimbal that follows life; static for quiet moments.
3. Environmental storytelling: places with people in them; details that say where and when (signs, food, weather, light).
4. Portrait framing: 35-85 mm-equivalent at a respectful distance; eye level; natural light by a window or in open shade.
5. Candid moments and expressions: be ready, long lens or hidden position, wait for the reaction.
6. Lifestyle B-roll: hands, textures, transport, food, sounds - a shot list by category, not by script line.
7. Travel structure: arrival (wide, new), immersion (medium, details), people (portraits), reflection (slow, golden hour), departure (callback).

## Defaults
| Item | Default |
|---|---|
| Light | natural; golden hour for emotional beats; avoid noon top light on faces |
| Movement | handheld/gimbal follow; slow reveals of places; no drone-on-every-shot |
| DOF | moderate; shallow for portraits and details |
| Sound | J-cuts of ambient sound into new places |
| Edit | match cuts on movement, music-led montage for days |

## Avoid
Over-graded looks that hide the real place; staged smiles; constant movement; the same gimbal walk-in every scene.
