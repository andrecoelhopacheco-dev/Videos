# M05 Cinematic storytelling (drama, suspense, emotional narrative)

Auto-detect: "short film", "drama", "thriller", "scene", "suspense", "cinematic story".

## Priorities
1. Visual metaphors that return changed (motif register).
2. Suspense by the information ledger: show the threat before the character knows.
3. Controlled reveals: every reveal has a planned hide.
4. Dramatic framing: composition carries power and isolation (centre, negative space, frames within frames).
5. Motivated camera movement: strict motivation gate; stillness as tension.
6. Emotional close-ups saved for peaks.
7. Lighting contrast as an arc (rises with tension).
8. Visual rhythm and scene progression: sequence patterns vary; holds after peaks.

## Defaults
| Item | Default |
|---|---|
| Lens identity | one family per film; a change marks a turn |
| Movement | <= 40% moving shots; slow push-ins only on true turns; one dolly zoom max |
| DOF | shallow up to 50% (emotional CUs), deep for staging in depth |
| Light | motivated, low-key for tension, contrast 4:1 to 8:1+ at peaks (convention) |
| Colour | a colour script with a motif colour |

## Avoid
Style over story; constant slow-motion; Dutch angles without a broken world; "epic" crane moves on small moments.
