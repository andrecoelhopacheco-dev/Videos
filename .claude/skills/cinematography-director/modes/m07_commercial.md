# M07 Commercial and product video (ads, product showcases, launches)

Auto-detect: "product", "ad", "commercial", "launch", "brand film", "showcase".

## Priorities
1. Product hero shot: the product dominates, lit to show form, at the peak of the piece.
2. Controlled camera movements: slider, turntable, motion-control or keyframed orbits - smooth, repeatable, eased.
3. Product lighting: shape (gradients on curved surfaces), edges (rims), materials (texture by raking light).
4. Material details: macro inserts of texture, finish, mechanism.
5. Reflections: control what reflects (flags, large soft sources, black cards); clean, intentional highlights.
6. Visual hierarchy: product > benefit > brand; text and logo in the end card.
7. Product reveal: hide-reveal pair (darkness, silhouette, partial frame, rotation) before the hero.

## Defaults
| Item | Default |
|---|---|
| Movement | slow orbits/arcs and push-ins with designed end frames; up to 70% moving |
| Lens | 85-135 mm for compression and clean backgrounds; macro for detail |
| DOF | shallow on details; deeper on the hero so the whole product is sharp |
| Light | soft key + strip lights for edges; high-key or dark premium by brand |
| Colour | brand palette; product colour accurate (TECHNICAL) |

## Avoid
Distorted product shapes (wide lens close); random flares; motion that hides the product; inaccurate product colour.
