# M01 Short-form vertical (YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikTok)

Auto-detect: duration <= 3 min; 9:16; words "Short", "Reel", "TikTok", "vertical".
Combine with a genre mode (M06 for tech explainers, M05 for story, M07 for product).

## Priorities
1. A visual hook in the first 1-2 s: the most surprising true image of the video, or a question made visible. No logo intros.
2. One idea per shot; the image is readable at phone size in under 1 s.
3. Fast progression: each new sentence or idea earns a new picture or a new framing.
4. Narration sync: cuts and moves land on word starts (measured times).
5. Vertical framing: centre and vertical stacking; faces and text in the central safe area; house rulebook values win. YouTube Help (checked 2026-11-05) states Shorts uploads up to 3 minutes with a square or vertical ratio and publishes no overlay safe-zone numbers - third-party margins are conventions (S43, S26).
6. Retention: a visible change every 2-4 s (cut, move, reveal, graphic) - but the camera holds while text is read.
7. Loop or callback ending: the last frame echoes the first.

## Defaults (override with a reason)
| Item | Default |
|---|---|
| Sizes | MCU/CU heavy; ECU inserts for proof; wides only with a vertical shape |
| Movement | static or slow push (1.03-1.10 scale); max 40% of shots moving |
| Zoom | punch-ins for emphasis on key words (inside headroom) |
| DOF | deep for screens and diagrams; shallow <= 30% of shots |
| Shot length | 1.5-4 s; a hold of 1-2 s after the reveal |
| Transitions | hard cuts; match cuts for idea links; no decorative wipes |
| Colour | high-clarity, one accent colour for the core idea |

## Avoid
Wide establishing shots with small subjects; moving camera under captions; repeating the same punch-in every sentence; content in the bottom UI zone.
