# M08 Documentary and music video

Auto-detect: "interview", "documentary", "observational", "real people"; or a song, BPM or "music video".

## Documentary
Priorities:
1. Observational storytelling: the camera waits; long lens or unobtrusive position; do not direct reality.
2. Interviews: one main angle (MCU, eye level, subject looking just off-lens) + one second angle (CU or profile, >= 30° apart) for cuts; motivated, soft key; background that says who they are.
3. Environmental B-roll: place, work, hands, routines; shot in sequences (wide - medium - close) for editing.
4. Suspense in non-fiction (with M05): the information ledger decides what is withheld; never fake events.
5. Honesty: label reconstructions and AI imagery as such; no staged "real" moments.
Defaults: zoom lenses and handheld for observation; static for interviews; natural light; deep focus for places.

## Music video
Priorities:
1. Musical synchronisation: cut on phrases and accents, not on every beat; camera moves start/stop on musical events (S15: Doyle built movement on music).
2. Performance cinematography: cover the performer with a master, a moving camera, and close details (hands, mouth); repeat takes for coverage.
3. Visual concept: one idea or metaphor that evolves with the song structure (verse/chorus/bridge = visual sections).
4. Rhythm: shot length mirrors song energy; the bridge breaks the pattern.
Defaults: handheld/gimbal for energy, locked-off for graphic concepts; strong colour script tied to sections.

## Avoid
Documentary: interviews with no B-roll plan; music beds deciding the cuts of serious content. Music video: cutting on every beat; effects without the song's structure.
