# M02 Long-form (documentaries, YouTube videos, longer narratives)

Auto-detect: duration > 3 min; 16:9; "YouTube video", "episode", "documentary", "essay".

## Priorities
1. Narrative progression across chapters; each chapter has its own visual opening (establish) and closing (resolve).
2. Scene establishment: geography before fragments.
3. Visual continuity across long spans: identity rules (lens family, palette, camera behaviour) written once.
4. Controlled pacing: alternate dense and breathing sections; no constant high energy.
5. Shot variation: a sequence of 3-7 shots per idea, with different sizes and angles.
6. B-roll integration: B-roll shows what the narration cannot (process, consequence, place), not word-for-word illustration.
7. Visual callbacks between chapters; the ending answers the opening image.
8. Emotional development: the contrast arc (light, colour, closeness) follows the emotion timeline.

## Defaults
| Item | Default |
|---|---|
| Sizes | full range; return to a wide at each chapter start |
| Movement | up to 50% moving shots; slow, motivated; static for interviews and explanations |
| DOF | mixed; shallow for interview isolation, deep for places and processes |
| Shot length | 3-8 s B-roll; longer holds for emotion |
| Transitions | hard cuts; dissolves for time; fade to black only between chapters |

## Avoid
Monotone talking head without B-roll plan; B-roll unrelated to the words; repeating one sequence pattern per chapter.
