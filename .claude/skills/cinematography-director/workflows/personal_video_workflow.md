# Workflow: personal / travel video (real footage)

Personal videos are found, not staged. The plan is a list of opportunities, not a script.
1. Brief: who, where, what feeling the user wants to keep (one sentence), length, phone or camera.
2. Story shape: arrival -> immersion -> people -> reflection -> departure (M04). Adjust to the trip.
3. Shot opportunity list by category (place wides, details, hands, food, transport, portraits, candid reactions, golden-hour moments, sound moments). For each: lens feel, support (handheld/gimbal/tripod), time of day, what to wait for.
4. Portrait plan: 35-85 mm equivalent, eye level, open shade or window light, background that says where.
5. Capture rules: hold every shot 5-10 s; record the 3 sizes of each subject; record ambient sound; shoot 16:9 UHD if a vertical cut is planned (headroom 1.125x) or shoot vertical directly.
6. Edit plan (`adapters/edit_resolve.md`): select by emotion, sequence by the story shape, J-cuts of ambient sound, music-led montage only where there is no speech.
7. Keep it authentic: grade for the real light of the place; no fake smiles, no staged moments unless the user asks.
