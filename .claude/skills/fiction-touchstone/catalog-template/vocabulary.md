# Touchstones Vocabulary

Allowed tag values per facet. Agents use only these values; new ones are proposed and added with the user's approval. Keep definitions to one line.

To rename or merge a value, update every work file that uses it in the same change.

## craft
- `braided-timeline` — two or more timelines interleaved, each reframing the other
- `unreliable-narrator` — narrator's account diverges from what the reader comes to understand
- `withheld-premise` — the core speculative fact is revealed gradually, not stated up front
- `epistolary` — told through documents, letters, logs, or journals
- `frame-story` — a story told within a story
- `exposition-through-conflict` — worldbuilding delivered via argument, choice, or consequence

## tone
- `restrained` — emotion held under the surface; understatement
- `wry` — dry humor alongside seriousness
- `elegiac` — mournful, retrospective
- `warm-under-pressure` — kindness and humor persisting through hardship
- `clinical` — detached, precise register

## theme
- `grief`
- `faith-and-doubt`
- `memory-identity`
- `consciousness`
- `love`
- `ethics-of-technology`
- `belonging`

## character
- `gender-subversion` — complicates or subverts gender expectations
- `sincere-antagonist` — opposition from someone with sincere, coherent values
- `quiet-protagonist` — observant, internal, non-heroic lead
- `moral-compromise` — protagonist makes defensible but costly choices

## speculative
- `consciousness-upload`
- `generation-ship`
- `near-future-religion`
- `memory-editing`
- `cloning`
- `first-contact-without-aliens` — contact-style disruption from a human or technological source

## effect
The emotional result in the reader.
- `dread`
- `awe`
- `quiet-devastation` — grief that lands softly and stays
- `devastation-then-hope`
- `tenderness`
- `vertigo` — reality or identity suddenly reframed
- `catharsis`

## stance
- `emulate`
- `avoid`
- `mixed`
